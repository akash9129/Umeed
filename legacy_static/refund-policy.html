Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 29 October 2025

1. General Charitable Donations

Donations made voluntarily to Umeed Ki Kiran Foundation for causes such as Education Kits, Stray Animal Feeding, Medicine Kits, or General Charitable Support are considered non-refundable once processed, as funds are allocated promptly for on-ground procurement and distribution in Varanasi.

2. Erroneous or Duplicate Transactions

If an erroneous amount or unintended duplicate transaction occurs due to technical glitches or gateway timeout, please contact us within 5 business days of the transaction date with proof (transaction reference number, date, bank debited). Upon internal verification, the duplicate excess amount will be processed for refund back to the original source method within 7–10 working days.

3. Celebration Package Rescheduling & Cancellations

We understand that unexpected circumstances arise:

  • Rescheduling: You can reschedule your celebration to any future available date with no penalty, provided you inform us via WhatsApp at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled drive.
  • Cancellation: If a package is cancelled before food and cake procurement is initiated (at least 24 hours prior to event), you may request a refund or convert the sum into general food packet donations. If perishable items (custom cake, cooked meals) have already been prepared, the booking cannot be cancelled or refunded.

4. How to Request Assistance
