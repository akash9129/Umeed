Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 29 October 2025

1. Introduction

Umeed Ki Kiran Foundation ("Trust", "we", "our", or "us") is a registered public charitable trust having its registered base in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India (Trust Deed Date: 29 October 2025, PAN: AABTU5801C). We respect the privacy of our donors, well-wishers, and visitors.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect donor and visitor details including name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, billing/postal address, and occasion details (for celebration packages). When donating, transaction details are captured via verified payment gateways (such as Razorpay or UPI banking applications). We do not store credit/debit card numbers or net banking passwords on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized for:

  • Conducting the requested celebration drive or food distribution in Varanasi.
  • Delivering celebration video wishes, photographs, and event confirmations to your WhatsApp or email.
  • Issuing formal donation receipts and statutory audit records under trust regulations.
  • Responding to inquiries and volunteer requests.

4. Data Security & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade donor information to any commercial marketer or third party. Data is only shared with authorized service providers such as payment processing partners (Razorpay/Banks) strictly to execute transactions securely.

5. Media & Child Dignity

All videos and photographs captured during celebrations are recorded with the full awareness and consent of the participating children's guardians and instructors, maintaining utmost respect and dignity.
