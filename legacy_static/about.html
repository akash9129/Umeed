Our Heritage & Mission

Built on the soil of Varanasi, driven by compassion.

Umeed Ki Kiran Foundation was established to ensure that every underprivileged child in Kashi receives education, warmth, nourishment, and a genuine ray of hope.

The Origin Story

Why we started Umeed Ki Kiran

"[PLACEHOLDER: In the ancient alleyways and ghats of Varanasi, we noticed countless children full of bright potential but lacking basic stationery, meals, and mentorship. Umeed Ki Kiran was born out of a simple conviction: when families celebrate their happiness together with those less fortunate, despair turns into dignity. We formally registered the public trust on 29 October 2025 to create enduring grassroots change across education, digital literacy, and community care. - Kunal Singh, Managing Trustee]"

— Kunal Singh, Founder & Managing Trustee

Rather than making grand distant promises, we focus on concrete, verifiable local interventions. When someone sponsors a food drive or a birthday party, our volunteers carry out the celebration personally on the ground.

Charter Objectives

5 pillars of our trust deed

Registered in Varanasi with clear philanthropic aims for youth and community welfare.

1. Education

Providing free evening tuition, school stationery kits, bags, and remedial coaching for children from slum clusters and daily-wage families.

2. Computer Training

Equipping youth with essential digital literacy, keyboard skills, basic office applications, and internet awareness to bridge the digital divide.

3. Community Libraries

Establishing accessible book corners and reading spaces with storybooks, general knowledge, and moral value literature in Hindi and English.

4. Student Hostels

Planning safe, conducive residential hostel facilities for deserving students hailing from rural outskirts who lack study environments at home.

5. Charitable Community Support

Hunger relief meal packets, women's menstrual hygiene distribution, emergency medicine kits, and humane stray animal feeding across Varanasi.
